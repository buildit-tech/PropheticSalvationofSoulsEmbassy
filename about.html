About Our Church

A Prophetic Movement Raising Souls for Christ

Who We Are

Prophetic Salvation of Souls Embassy is a prophetic and revival ministry committed to winning souls, restoring destinies, and raising believers who walk in power, holiness, and divine purpose.

We are a prophetic movement where lives are transformed through the Word of God, prophetic insight, and the manifestation of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To raise a generation transformed by the power of God and prepared to impact the world for Christ.

Our Mission

Winning souls, restoring lives, and advancing the Kingdom of God through prophetic teaching, prayer, and revival encounters.

Meet Our Prophet

Prophet Bright Odiase

Prophet Bright Odiase is the visionary leader of Prophetic Salvation of Souls Embassy, called to raise a prophetic generation and lead people into deeper encounters with God.

Through prophetic ministry, teaching, and prayer, lives continue to experience healing, restoration, and transformation.

What We Believe

  • The Bible is the inspired Word of God.
  • Jesus Christ is Lord and Savior.
  • Salvation comes through faith in Christ.
  • The Holy Spirit empowers believers today.
  • Prayer and holiness are essential to Christian living.
